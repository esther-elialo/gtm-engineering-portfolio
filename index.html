Background

Who I am.

I spent 12+ years in B2B SaaS sales — as an Account Executive, Sales Manager, Head of Growth, and GTM Specialist. I know what it feels like to spend 3 hours researching one account before a call, to manually triage 200 inbound leads, to build pipeline from scratch without the right tools.
